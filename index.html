Quick rules for Tor

Ritual casting (Ritual Adept)Any spell with the Ritual tag in the spellbook can be cast without preparing it and without a slot — add 10 minutes to the casting time. Alarm every rest keeps the sand-shell up for free.
Arcane Ward (wizard 3+)Cast any abjuration spell with a slot and the ward appears with HP = 2×wizard level + Int mod. It absorbs damage before Tor's HP. Each abjuration cast recharges 2× slot level. Bonus action + slot rebuilds it directly. At 6: Projected Ward throws it over an ally within 30 ft as a reaction.
Short restsAt least 1 hour. Healing comes from spending Hit Dice (d6 + Con each, tracked on the Combat tab) — there is no automatic healing. Arcane Recovery (1/day): restore slots totaling half wizard level, rounded up, none 6th+; un-tap the pips yourself.
Long rests8 hours. HP to max, all slots back, Earth Tremor and Pass Without Trace reset, and you regain half your total Hit Dice (minimum 1).
ConcentrationOne concentration spell at a time (Fog Cloud, Create Bonfire, Pass Without Trace). Taking damage: Con save DC 10 or half the damage, whichever is higher. Tor saves at +4.
Desert heat (DMG extreme heat)Hourly Con saves vs exhaustion in extreme heat. Tor: +4 Con, no armor, Beckon Water for endless drinking water, Mold Earth for shade trenches, darkvision for night travel. The desert doesn't harm its own.
